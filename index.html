📖 How to play (click to hide)
GoalBe the last one standing. Clear lines to send garbage rows to your opponents.
Controls← → move, ↑ or X rotate, Z rotate back, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, Shift or C hold a piece. Controllers work too (click 🎮 by the board).Use the on screen pad: ◀ ▶ move, ▼ soft drop, ▲ or Y hard drop, A rotate, B rotate back, X hold a piece. Hold ◀ ▶ ▼ to repeat.
CombosClear lines with back to back pieces and your lines, score and garbage multiply by your streak.
Grey rowsGarbage rows never clear from normal line clears. Only a 4 line Blockfight removes one.
PowersEvery 5 lines sends 1 garbage row to a random opponent (2 rows from a phone). A 4 line Blockfight hits everyone with a row plus a random bonus: an extra row, or a flipped board with reversed controls for 8 seconds. It also clears a pure grey garbage row from your own stack. Making two Blockfights in a row also scrambles every opponent's board.
SpeedBlocks fall faster every 20 seconds, and the timer flashes when it happens.
RoomsThe first player in is the host and starts the game. With 4 or more players anyone can call a vote kick.
